01OUR PURPOSE

A bridge built
through people.

China Cultural Centre in Iraq is envisioned as a welcoming platform for cultural discovery, language learning and collaboration between communities.

Based in Sulaymaniyah, we bring Chinese culture closer to audiences in Iraq while creating space for dialogue, creativity and enduring cross-cultural relationships.
